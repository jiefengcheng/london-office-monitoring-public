Search by the named entity. Inspect dated project observations and retain their
status, source and original locator. Planning approval, pre-letting, structural
completion, practical completion, fit-out and occupation are different stages.

Select original fact items to show a timeline; do not replace old forecasts with
later observations. A changed historical area figure is not automatically a
physical expansion. Completed or pre-let area is not automatically newly
available supply. Preserve the denominator of each proportion, including ERV
versus floor area and scheme count versus floorspace. This skill retrieves and
presents evidence; it does not implement a refresh or ingestion operation.
